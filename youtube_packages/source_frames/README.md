# Source stills for the Riwaq publishing covers

Each JPG here is a crop from the existing, approved thumbnail for the corresponding final cut in the previous Drive delivery. The stills are real frames from the source video; they are not AI-generated and no faces were synthesized.

- Long cuts: cropped from the right-hand source-frame panel of the 1280×720 project thumbnail (`x=566..1279`, `y=0..719`).
- Shorts: cropped from the central 16:9 video panel of the 1080×1920 project thumbnail (`x=184..895`, `y=535..934`). This deliberately omits the baked-in captions and lower broadcast overlay while retaining the actual photographed person/frame.

The source thumbnails remain on Google Drive; these small, unbranded stills are committed so the final cover designs can be rebuilt without copying any MP4 into Git. Original Drive thumbnail IDs are in `../package_manifest.json`.
