## B. Spatial design system
1. **Canvas:** 16:9; deliver 1920×1080, 30fps. Build at 1920×1080 logical units and test at half scale.
2. **Background:** deep dark navy/graphite with gradient atmospherics, soft flowing cyan and warm coral/gold light pools near edges; central legibility remains highest.
3. **Object surfaces:** glossy dark metallic hero objects, realistic glass reflections, lightly translucent floating cards, rounded but restrained radii, 1px illuminated keyline, believable soft contact shadows.
4. **Lighting:** one principal warm-gold/orange highlight, one cool cyan rim; background halos must sit BEHIND objects, not cover text.
5. **Hierarchy:** one dominant object or headline per composition; supporting numbers/labels are intentionally smaller but fully readable on mobile; preserve negative space.
6. **Transitions:** content-transform (same object evolves into next concept), directional glide, fast mask or light sweep; avoid arbitrary spin, zoom-only loops, overused explosion effects.
7. **Type:** short 1–6 word lines, excellent white/ivory contrast, gold for a SINGLE relevant stress word; Arabic RTL correctly shaped. Never recreate the reference's English text or proprietary GUI.
8. **Audio linkage:** every new label/attention event timed to a spoken cue; silence/pauses can be used for dramatic motion without inventing VO.
9. **UI:** layered data cards are chosen only when they represent evidence or interpretation, NOT fake software screens.
